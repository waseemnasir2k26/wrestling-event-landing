All ages welcome. Under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Some matches contain mature content.

Are refunds available? +

All ticket sales are final. If the event is canceled, you'll be refunded automatically through Eventbrite.

Can I bring a camera? +

Phones and point-and-shoot cameras yes. Professional cameras, tripods, and flash require a press pass — email us.

Is re-entry allowed? +

Yes — hand stamp required. Keep it visible for door staff.

How do I buy merch at the show? +

Cash and all major cards accepted at the merch table. Wrestlers sign at intermission.
